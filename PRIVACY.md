# Privacy Policy: WebNovel Ultimate Chapter Copier

Last updated: October 20, 2026

WebNovel Ultimate Chapter Copier ("the extension") copies chapter text from supported websites to your clipboard. This policy explains what data the extension handles.

## Data processed on your device

Chapter text is read from the page you are viewing and processed locally in your browser. It is written to your clipboard when you copy it. The extension does not save chapter text.

## Data stored in your browser

- Preferences such as Auto-Copy, Ctrl + A, copy format, notifications, extraction timeout, and your list of custom sites are stored in `chrome.storage.local`.
- For the current tab, the extension keeps short-lived values in `sessionStorage`, such as the last chapter ID and a signature of its text. These are cleared when the tab closes.

## Data we collect or share

We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data or chapter text. The extension does not send information to any server operated by the developer or by third parties.

## Permissions

- **Storage:** saves your settings.
- **Clipboard write and offscreen:** copy text to your clipboard.
- **Scripting:** runs the extension on custom sites you add.
- **Optional site access:** requested only when you add a custom site, one site at a time. Removing a site also removes its access.

## Third-party sites

The extension works on websites you visit. Their privacy policies and terms of service apply to your use of those sites.
